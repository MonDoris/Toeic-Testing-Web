## 1. Chức năng giao tiếp

| Chức năng | Mẫu câu | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| Yêu cầu lịch sự | Could / Would you + V? | **Could you** send me the file? |
| Xin phép | May / Can I + V? | **May I** use your phone? |
| Đề nghị giúp | Shall I / Should I + V? | **Should I** book a taxi for you? |
| Gợi ý | We could / Why don't we + V? | **Why don't we** meet on Friday? |
| Lời khuyên | should / ought to | You **should** arrive early. |
| Bắt buộc | must / have to | Visitors **must** sign in at the front desk. |
| Khả năng | may / might / could | The flight **might** be delayed. |

## 2. Mẹo Part 3 – câu hỏi về “yêu cầu / đề nghị”
- *“What does the woman **ask** the man to do?”* → nghe câu có **Could you / Can you / Please**.
- *“What does the man **offer** to do?”* → nghe **I'll… / Let me… / Shall I… / I can…**
- *“What does the woman **suggest**?”* → nghe **Why don't you… / You should… / How about…**

## 3. Áp dụng email (Writing Q6–7)
- Yêu cầu: ***Could you please*** *let me know the price?*
- Đề nghị: ***I would suggest*** *that we move the meeting to 3 p.m.*
- Xin lỗi & giải pháp: *We apologize for the delay. We **will** send a replacement immediately.*
