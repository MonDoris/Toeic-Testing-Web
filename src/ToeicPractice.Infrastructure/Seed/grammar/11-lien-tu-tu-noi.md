## 1. Liên từ phụ thuộc (nối 2 mệnh đề trong CÙNG một câu)

| Ý nghĩa | Liên từ | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| Nguyên nhân | because, since, as | She is smiling **because** she got good news. |
| Nhượng bộ | although, even though, while | **Although** it is raining, people are walking outside. |
| Mục đích | so that, in order that | He is using a ladder **so that** he can reach the shelf. |
| Thời gian | when, while, before, after, until, as soon as | A man is reading **while** he waits for the train. |
| Điều kiện | if, unless, as long as | **Unless** you register, you can't attend. |

## 2. Trạng từ liên kết (nối 2 câu)
**However, Therefore, Moreover, In addition, For example, As a result, On the other hand**
- *The product is expensive. **However**, it is very popular.*
- Dùng dấu chấm phẩy: *The venue was full**; therefore,** we moved outside.*

## 3. Giới từ ≠ Liên từ
| Giới từ (+ danh từ/V-ing) | Liên từ (+ mệnh đề) |
|---|---|
| **because of** the rain | **because** it rained |
| **despite / in spite of** the cost | **although** it is costly |
| **during** the meeting | **while** we were meeting |

## 4. Mẹo Writing Q1–5
Đề cho **because / although / so** → câu phải có **2 mệnh đề**:
*The woman is holding an umbrella **because** it is raining.*
